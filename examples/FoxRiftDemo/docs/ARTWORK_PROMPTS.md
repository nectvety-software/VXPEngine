## Arena → assets/source/arena.png

Create a game background asset only, no text, no UI, no characters. Portrait 3:4 composition for a 240x320 mobile pixel MOBA inspired by icy fantasy arenas. Isometric overhead 3/4 view of blue-gray stone paving in snowy ruins. Large round blue glowing crystal nexus with antique bronze ring on upper right, snow-covered fractured fortress walls upper left. Walkable slate blue stone lane sweeps from lower left to upper right, central and lower area relatively open for actors. Dramatic cyan crystal lighting, detailed low-resolution pixel-art texture, crisp deliberate pixel clusters, no blur. Fill entire portrait image, no margins. Intended runtime uses top24 and bottom64 pixels for HUD.


Arena is retained as original unbranded environmental artwork. The old fox character sheet has been replaced.
