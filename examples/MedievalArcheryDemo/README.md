# Arrowfall Duel — Medieval Archery VXP sample

A standalone VXPEngine sample project. Two archers duel across a scrolling medieval battlefield. The player sets angle and power; gravity and round-specific wind drive the same projectile simulation used by the AI.

## Controls

- Up / Down: angle
- Hold A or OK: charge the power bar (it sweeps 30–90 and back)
- Release A or OK: fire with the shown power
- Left / Right: fine power trim
- Touch (pen) devices: hold the screen to charge, drag up/down to adjust angle, release to fire
- Left soft key or `0`: Start / pause
- Right soft key / Back: back or exit

The first archer to win two rounds wins the match. Body hits deal 30 HP and headshots deal 50 HP. Releasing the charge at power 84+ lands a full draw: 45 body / 65 head damage. While aiming, a marker shows where the arrow will land (wind included) and flashes `LOCKED BODY` / `LOCKED HEAD` when the shot would hit the boss. Choose Easy, Normal, or Hard on the title screen.

### Tactical minimap

A minimap strip sits right under the score bar during play. It compresses the whole battlefield (world x 0..540) into one band:

- Green square: your archer. Red square: the boss. Tick marks every 100 world units.
- Yellow marker: predicted landing point of your current shot, wind included. It turns cream (body) or green (head) when the trajectory would strike the boss.
- `L n` / `R+n` readout: how many world units your shot lands short (left) or long (right) of the boss — tune angle/power until it reads `0` to lock a hit.
- Teal chevrons: wind direction and strength; the row drifts as wind pushes arrows during flight.
- The light-gray bracket shows where the main camera is looking.

### Visuals

The battlefield is fully procedural — no extra assets, feature-phone friendly:

- Parallax sky with a glowing sun and three wind-drifting cloud layers; the tree line and distant castle scroll at their own depths.
- Wind-blown leaves tumble across the field so you can read the wind in-world, not just from the HUD.
- Impact feedback: screen shake, white hit flash, and a particle burst (gold sparks on headshots, dust puffs on misses). Archers blink white while taking damage.
- Health bars have a white "ghost" trail that drains slowly, showing exactly how much the last shot took.
- Arrows are two-tone (gold for yours, cream for the boss's) with a fading trail; the landing marker pulses while you aim.

Run `build_arm.bat`, or `run_vxpemu.bat` to build and launch `build-archery/main/arrowfall_duel.vxp`.

The checked-in placeholder artwork is generated by `tools/create_placeholder_assets.py`, so the sample is immediately buildable. `assets/spritefusion-plan.json` records the production prompts and replacement slots. With a Sprite Fusion key in `.env`, run `tools/spritefusion_generate.py`, then rebuild; generated PNGs are preserved and converted to fixed-size VXP RAW resources. Never ship or commit the API key.
